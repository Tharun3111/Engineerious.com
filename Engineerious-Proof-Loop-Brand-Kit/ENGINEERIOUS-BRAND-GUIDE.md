# Engineerious — Proof Loop Brand Guide

## 1. Confirmed brief

**Purpose:** Help engineers and founders build production-grade AI systems that are evaluated, observable, and reliable rather than merely impressive in demos.

**Primary audience:** Startup founders and hands-on technical builders. **Credibility audience:** Investors, engineering leaders, and production ML practitioners.

**Values:** Trust, innovation, simplicity, transparency, and production discipline.

**Constraints:** Legible at 16–32px, reproducible in one color, color-accessible, no gradients, no thin strokes, no generic AI imagery, and no dependence on a metaphor that requires explanation.

## 2. Final concept: Proof Loop

The mark combines a continuous circular loop with one square checkpoint. The loop represents human feedback, iteration, and evaluation; the square represents the explicit production gate where evidence replaces intuition. It avoids brains, robots, circuit traces, sparkles, arrows, and other category clichés.

The wordmark uses a sturdy humanist-style sans-serif construction. The supplied SVG wordmark is converted to paths, so it does not require a font installation.

### Geometry

- Two components only: one open loop and one square checkpoint.
- Gap centered in the upper-right quadrant to imply an intentional decision point, not a loading spinner.
- Square side equals roughly one loop stroke plus optical compensation.
- No rounded corners: the contrast between circular feedback and square rigor carries the idea.

## 4. Formal review plan

### Review personas

- **Maya Chen — YC-style reviewer:** Tests whether a founder can use the identity in a browser tab, pitch deck, product header, and demo without explaining it.
- **Product CEO:** Evaluates strategic fit, trust, and whether the identity can grow beyond a personal brand.
- **Investor CEO:** Evaluates credibility, memorability after a short exposure, and presentation quality in funding contexts.
- **Design systems lead:** Evaluates geometry, exports, accessibility, implementation, and cross-platform consistency.

These personas are fictional review lenses, not endorsements from YC or named executives.

### Scoring criteria

Use a 1–5 score for clarity, memorability, 16–32px scalability, monochrome performance, production feasibility, accessibility, and non-generic distinctiveness. Collect one mandatory written risk and one recommended change from each reviewer. Do not average away a critical favicon, accessibility, or trademark concern.

### Review package

- SVG primary, dark, monochrome, and icon-only files.
- PNG 1x/2x exports plus 16px, 32px, and 64px favicon tests.
- One-page contact sheet.
- CSV rubric with scores and comments.
- Reviewers inspect files at 100% scale; they do not score enlarged screenshots alone.

## 5. Feedback synthesis workflow

1. Normalize each comment into **observation → evidence → severity → proposed action**.
2. Group feedback under recognition, geometry, typography, color, accessibility, and implementation.
3. Calculate weighted means, but automatically escalate any score below 3 for small-size clarity, monochrome, accessibility, or feasibility.
4. Convert repeated comments into one design hypothesis, such as “the checkpoint merges into the loop at 16px.”
5. Change one variable per iteration—gap, square size, stroke, spacing, or wordmark weight—and rerun the same tests.
6. Freeze geometry only after two consecutive review rounds produce no critical failures.

Current fictional panel result: **4.54/5 weighted**. Main residual risk: a circular loop can resemble a status/loading symbol if animated or rotated; usage rules therefore prohibit rotation and loading animation.

## 6. Usage guidelines

### Clear space

Maintain clear space on every side equal to the checkpoint square width. No text, border, photograph edge, or interface control may enter this area.

### Minimum size

- Icon: 16px digital; 6mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 120px digital; 28mm print.
- At 16px, use the primary or monochrome icon only—never the horizontal wordmark.

### Do

- Use exact supplied artwork.
- Prefer the primary light lockup on white or the dark lockup on `#111827`.
- Use monochrome when printing with one ink or when color reproduction is uncertain.
- Keep the checkpoint in the upper-right position.

### Do not

- Rotate or animate the loop as a loading indicator.
- Add arrows, checkmarks, glow, gradients, shadows, outlines, or extra nodes.
- Recolor the green checkpoint as red in normal brand use.
- Stretch, skew, crop, or rearrange the components.
- Place the primary blue/green mark on busy imagery without a solid holding area.

## Stakeholder rationale

Engineerious uses a continuous loop interrupted by one square checkpoint: human feedback remains continuous, but production release is deliberate and evidence-based. The two-element mark is memorable, works in one color, stays legible at favicon size, and avoids the visual clichés commonly associated with AI brands.

## Legal note

The package is design-ready, not trademark-cleared. Conduct a professional wordmark and visual-similarity search before public launch.
